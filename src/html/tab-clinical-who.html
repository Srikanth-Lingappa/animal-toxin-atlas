What this shows. The WHO Guidelines for the production, control and regulation of snake antivenom immunoglobulins (Annex 5, TRS No. 1004) list the snake species of greatest medical importance in four broad world regions — the species responsible for the most frequent or severe snakebite envenoming, and the starting point for regional antivenom-production priorities. This view cross-references that list against this dataset by organism name, so you can see, region by region, which medically important species already have a characterized Tox-Prot venom proteome and which have no characterized toxin entries at all — a real, public-health-relevant venomics gap list, distinct from the Structural Gaps view in Insights. Species are matched by binomial name only; taxonomic synonyms or recent splits may occasionally cause a miss.
