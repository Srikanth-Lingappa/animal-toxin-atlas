# Animal Toxin Atlas

An interactive browser for exploring animal toxins, their sequence and functional annotations, structural features, taxonomy, and relationships.

## Overview

Animal Toxin Atlas brings together curated protein records from UniProt's Tox-Prot programme in a single interface for browsing, filtering, comparing and exploring animal toxins.

The project aims to make toxin annotations and sequence-level information more accessible, while connecting individual proteins to their biological context, structural evidence, and potential clinical relevance.

The current dataset comprises 8,274 reviewed Tox-Prot records selected from Metazoa entries carrying tissue-specificity annotations. Additional views integrate selected external classifications and computational analyses.

## Features

- Browse and filter curated animal toxin records
- Explore organisms, taxonomy and inferred toxin classes
- Inspect precursor sequences, mature chains, cleavage sites and post-translational modifications
- Examine disulfide bonds and physicochemical properties
- Explore InterPro annotations and structural cross-references
- Investigate medically important venomous snakes using a WHO-derived species overlay
- Visualise protein-language-model embedding maps
- Search sequence similarity and explore selected toxin relationships

## Data and methodology

The underlying protein records originate from UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot's Tox-Prot programme. Additional classifications, overlays and computational analyses are documented within the atlas.

Derived classifications and computational results should be distinguished from original curated annotations. Please consult the atlas's About section for data selection, methods, limitations and source references.

## Development status

**Work in progress.**

The atlas is actively being developed. Features, classifications, visualisations and interpretations may change as the project evolves. Derived results should be independently checked before being used for research or other consequential decisions.
