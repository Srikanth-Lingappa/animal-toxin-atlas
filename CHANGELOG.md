# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — first public release

- 8,274 reviewed UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot venom protein records (export of 26 Sep 2026).
- Browse, Organism, Taxonomy, Disulfides, Clinical Importance, Toxin Relationships, Similarity Search, Insights
  (structural gaps, potency and lethality), Statistics and About.

- ProtT5 and ProstT5 embedding maps.
- Full verification against the UniProt export (`docs/VERIFICATION.md`).
